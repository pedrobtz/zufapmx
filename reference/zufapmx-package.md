# zufapmx: Read, Write and Check Equivalence of Pharmacometric Models

Holds a population pharmacokinetic and pharmacodynamic model as one R
object, reads it from 'NONMEM' control streams, 'rxode2' and 'nlmixr2'
model functions and 'mrgsolve' model specifications, and writes it to
any of them, reporting what a translation keeps and what it loses.
Checks that two models are the same model by simulating both on a
canonical design, each through its own engine, and comparing the
predictions against a tolerance.
